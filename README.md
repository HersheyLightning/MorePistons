# Description
More Pistons adds a variety of unique piston types to the game. It adds honey pistons, strong pistons, long pistons of lengths 2 - 8, and smart pistons. It's also compatible with Carpet mod's movable block entities.

# Features

## Honey Piston
<img src="https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/VMLvCf4v/images/250143a24ea0a04d4ef8d2d33cea6dbd25794fab.png" alt="Extended honey piston" height="100px">

Honey pistons act exactly like sticky pistons, but will never spit out their block. They are crafted with a honey bottle instead of a slime ball.

## Strong Piston
<img src="https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/VMLvCf4v/images/ce765f67d377f364189649a2cfd7619386da3771.png" alt="Extended strong piston" height="100px">

Strong pistons are the same as normal pistons but can push twice the amount of blocks. They are crafted the same way as a piston, but the bottom left and right cobblestone is replaced with obsidian and the iron ingot is replaced with netherite.

## Long Pistons
<img src="https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/VMLvCf4v/images/cf638308c269bc8f973eee111b8b6cf4c726a82f.png" alt="Extended pistons from length 1 to 8" height="300px">

Long pistons come in lengths between 2 - 8 and take the same amount of time to move per block as regular pistons. To craft the next length of extension, put the previous length piston, iron ingot, and 3 planks in a T shape. For a strong piston, replace the iron ingot with netherite.

## Smart piston
<img src="https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/VMLvCf4v/images/7cb0e1d2aa02d6c0b8039151d23bbfe638c0bea4.png" alt="A comparator connected to a smart piston that powers redstone lamps" height="200px">

Smart pistons take in a specific signal strength and extend to that amount roughly divided by 2. If you put in 15 signal strength, the piston will extend 8 blocks and if you put in 1 signal strength the piston will extend 1 block. These pistons are crafted the same way as normal pistons but the left and right cobblestone is replaced with quartz and the redstone dust is replaces with an octuple piston.
